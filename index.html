My name is Lada Schröderová and I am currently studying Digital Concept Development at Business Academy Aarhus. I’m from the Czech Republic and grew up in an environment where my parents own two companies - one specializing in snowboards (LTB snowboards) and the other in sewing clothing (Salebra). My older brother is also a snowboard maker and he is trying to find sustainable ways in the industry through his small project Second Chance for Material. My sister focused on sewing and creating upcycled pieces. I showed interest in the theoretical side of the process and its sustainability from different perspectives. I grew up in a creative environment, surrounded by people doing what they enjoy and trying to minimize the negative impact on our planet. I try to share the same mindset.

My interest is new trends and topics in the fashion industry, and I am passionate about improving myself in this sector. I also have experience in organizing and managing events, including fashion shows. Previously, I worked as a producer at the start-up BAIM, where we organised events and clothing launches for upcoming brands. In addition, I did promotion and content creating for a small festival in the Czech Republic.
